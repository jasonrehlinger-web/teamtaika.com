Our NASPO ValuePoint affiliation

A competitively-awarded contract every participating state can use.

NASPO ValuePoint is the cooperative purchasing arm of the National Association of State Procurement Officials (NASPO). Its master agreements are solicited and awarded by a lead state on behalf of all participating states — so once a state joins, its agencies and eligible local entities can buy without repeating the procurement. Taika's master agreement (40-00000-24-00076AJ, Remote Interpreting and Translating Services) was awarded through lead state New Mexico, and our listing is published in the NASPO ValuePoint vendor portfolio.
