HB 22 — Limited English Access to State Programs (2022)

New Mexico requires cabinet-level agencies to collect language-use data and implement plans giving LEP residents meaningful access to state services.

Source: New Mexico Legislature — HB 22 (2022)

New Mexico Courts Language Access Services

The Administrative Office of the Courts recruits, trains, certifies, and funds court interpreters so LEP and Deaf/hard-of-hearing New Mexicans have equal court access.
