# NOTE: Googlebot/Bingbot deliberately have NO group of their own. A robots.txt
# crawler obeys ONLY its most-specific matching group, so a "User-agent: Googlebot"
# block with just "Allow: /" would SHADOW the Disallow rules above and let Google
# crawl /.netlify/functions/ etc. (that caused a "Blocked due to 4xx" issue in GSC).
# With no dedicated group, Googlebot/Bingbot fall back to "*" and honor the Disallows.
